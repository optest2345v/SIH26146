## Public benchmark / datasets

### Elliptic Bitcoin transaction dataset

Useful for blockchain-side illicit-transaction benchmarking. It contains labeled and unlabeled transaction data but does not provide the complete SIH network telemetry schema.

Reference: https://www.kaggle.com/ellipticco/elliptic-data-set

### Bitcoin P2P research dataset

Useful for understanding network-layer Bitcoin measurements and source-inference research.

Reference: https://concordia-btc-p2p.lhs.inria.fr/

## Research

### Deanonymization in the Bitcoin P2P Network

Relevant to understanding why network-layer observations can provide probabilistic source information and why IP evidence should not automatically be treated as identity proof.

Reference: https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2017/hash/6c3cf77d52820cd0fe646d38bc2145ca-Abstract.html
